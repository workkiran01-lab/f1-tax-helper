## Tax rules and sources

The app screens common cases and asks for review when facts are insufficient. A calendar-year count is not an unconditional residency determination. Student exclusions depend on qualifying visa history and compliance, including prior nonconsecutive exempt years. The IRS distinguishes student rules from teacher/trainee rules. [IRS student exclusions](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/exempt-individual-who-is-a-student), [substantial presence test](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/substantial-presence-test).

The IRS final Form 8843 currently available is for 2025; the 2026 version is a draft marked not for filing. The corrected prior-year generator uses the final 2025 AcroForm, including actual current-year excluded days, history, and the permanent-residence application questions. Its file fingerprint is locked in code. [Final Form 8843](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8843.pdf), [2026 draft](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-dft/f8843--dft.pdf).

Under current individual Form 1040-NR instructions, employee wages subject to US income tax withholding generally use the fourth-month deadline; otherwise the sixth-month deadline applies. For calendar tax year 2026, the app displays expected April 15 or June 15, 2027 dates and a provisional-date notice. Standalone 8843 generally follows the June deadline when no income tax return is required. Filing extensions do not extend payment deadlines. State requirements and relief may differ. [1040-NR instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040nr), [Form 8843 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f8843.pdf).

Expected furnishing dates are February 1, 2027 for W-2s because January 31 is a Sunday, and March 15, 2027 for Form 1042-S. Final instructions and extensions must still be checked. [W-2/W-3 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw2w3), [1042-S instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1042s).

Eligible Indian students or business apprentices under the treaty can have a standard-deduction exception; the single base amount is $15,750 for tax year 2025 and $16,100 for tax year 2026. This is not a deduction for all nonresident students. [1040-NR instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040nr), [IRS tax-year 2026 adjustments](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-releases-tax-inflation-adjustments-for-tax-year-2026-including-amendments-from-the-one-big-beautiful-bill).

Treaty treatment depends on residence and the applicable article and conditions. Form 8833 has disclosure exceptions, including several student/teacher and scholarship situations. Form 8233 and W-8BEN serve different withholding purposes. Relevant Russian treaty provisions were suspended effective August 16, 2024, and the Hungary treaty ceased to have effect January 1, 2024. Other country records must be reviewed individually before adding automatic benefit claims. [IRS treaty directory](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/united-states-income-tax-treaties-a-to-z), [Publication 901](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p901), [treaty disclosure](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/claiming-tax-treaty-benefits), [scholarship withholding](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/claiming-treaty-exemption-for-a-scholarship-or-fellowship-grant).

- This audit did not run a live-browser/end-to-end session or verify live Supabase, Groq, Redis, Resend, Vercel, or Sentry configuration. Generated AI tax answers cannot be guaranteed correct by unit tests.
